Soner Berşe

Associate Professor of Nursing, PhD, RN

I head the Division of Fundamentals of Nursing at Gaziantep University. My research examines how nursing students learn clinical skills, how we assess those skills and how nurses and students use artificial intelligence.

Research

My work brings together clinical simulation, objective structured clinical examinations (OSCEs), digital learning and psychometrics. With colleagues, I develop research instruments and study how they perform across languages and clinical settings.

How ready are nursing students to use AI?

In a TÜBİTAK-supported student project, our team worked with 416 nursing students to develop a 20-item measure of AI readiness across four domains. Student researcher Gülay Koca contributed to the research question, data collection and the first manuscript draft. Read the study in BMC Nursing.
